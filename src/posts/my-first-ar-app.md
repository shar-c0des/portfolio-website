The journey of creating Roomantics has been one of the most challenging and rewarding experiences of my development career. When I first conceptualized this app, I was excited about the possibilities of AR technology but had no idea how complex the implementation would be.

## The Concept

Roomantics started as a simple idea: what if people could visualize furniture and decor in their spaces before making purchasing decisions? The name comes from combining "Room" and "Romantics" - because decorating your space should be a love story, not a frustrating experience.

![AR Interface Preview](/assets/blog/ar-interface.png)

## Technical Challenges

### AR Implementation
The biggest challenge was implementing AR functionality that worked reliably across different devices. I chose Flutter for the cross-platform capabilities, which meant finding the right AR library that could handle:
- Surface detection
- Object placement and scaling
- Real-time lighting adjustments
- Performance optimization

### Spatial Understanding
Getting the spatial understanding right was crucial. The app needed to:
- Accurately detect room boundaries
- Understand furniture scale relative to the space
- Provide realistic lighting and shadows
- Handle edge cases like curved walls or unusual room shapes

## What I Learned

### AR Development Best Practices
1. **Start with simple interactions** - I initially tried to implement everything at once, which led to a confusing user experience
2. **Optimize for performance** - AR apps are resource-intensive, so every optimization matters
3. **Test on real devices early** - Emulators can only take you so far with AR functionality

### User Experience Design
The UI had to be intuitive enough for non-technical users while providing advanced features for design enthusiasts. This meant creating:
- Simple onboarding flows
- Progressive disclosure of features
- Clear visual feedback for AR interactions

## Key Features Implemented

- **Real-time AR visualization**
- **Furniture catalog with accurate scaling**
- **Room measurement tools**
- **Lighting simulation**
- **Screenshot and sharing capabilities**

## Lessons for Future Projects

This project taught me the importance of:
- User research in AR/VR contexts
- Performance optimization from day one
- Testing across diverse device specifications
- Building for accessibility in spatial interfaces

The app is still evolving, but I'm proud of what we've accomplished so far. Each update brings new features and improvements based on user feedback.

## Next Steps

Future enhancements planned include:
- AI-powered furniture recommendations
- Collaborative design sessions
- Integration with e-commerce platforms
- Advanced customization options

Building Roomantics has been an incredible learning experience, and I'm excited to continue pushing the boundaries of what's possible with AR in interior design.
